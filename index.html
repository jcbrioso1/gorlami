The back office your company never had.

You talk to one person: Gorlami. Behind that person, a small team of specialists reads your mail, keeps promises from slipping, watches the calendar and files the documents. You only hear about the decisions that need you.

Emily · Mail

Keeps the inbox clean as mail arrives. Labels, files, drafts replies, and raises anything that needs a human answer.

Chase · Follow-through

Tracks every "I'll send it Friday" and every unpaid invoice until it is actually done.

Callie · Calendar

Turns a thread of "Thursday?" and "3pm works" into one hold, and never double-books.

Prue · Documents

Finds the right file, reads it, and cites where the answer came from.

How it stays safe

Gorlami works inside limits you set. Routine work happens quietly. Anything that leaves the company — sending, spending, deleting, granting new access — waits for your approval, and the approval shows exactly what a yes will do before you give it. Every action is recorded so you can always see what got done, what is in motion, and what is waiting on you.

Who it is for

Small and mid-size companies that need an office to run while the owner is on a roof, in a courtroom, or with a customer. One Gorlami per company. Extra people get windows into the same office, not separate assistants that don't talk to each other.
