The Gorlami web reader

User-Agent: Gorlami/2.1 (+https://jcbrioso1.github.io/gorlami/bot)

Gorlami is a back-office assistant for small businesses. When a business owner using Gorlami is working on something — answering a customer, checking a supplier, looking something up — Gorlami may read a single web page on that owner's behalf: a page linked in an email they received, a page they named, or a search result.

What it reads, and how

  • Single pages, on behalf of one business owner, one at a time, when that owner is working on something. It is not a crawler and does not index the web.
  • A plain request: one GET, no cookies, no logins, no forms, and no JavaScript. It never submits anything and never follows a link that would do something (unsubscribe, confirm, sign in, pay) or carries a token.
  • A business's own website, when that business uses Gorlami: a few of its pages at setup, and again about once a week, to keep its own facts (address, hours, services) up to date.
  • What it reads is used to answer the owner who asked. Pages are not republished.

Opting out

Gorlami honours robots.txt rules that name it. To ask Gorlami not to read your site, add:

User-agent: Gorlami
Disallow: /

Because Gorlami reads single pages for a person rather than crawling, it treats rules for all robots (User-agent: *) as meant for crawlers when it reads a single page an owner asked for. The weekly check of a business's own website follows robots.txt in full.
