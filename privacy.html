Privacy Policy

Gorlami · Effective September 29, 2026

Gorlami is a software service that helps a company run its back office: reading and organizing email, tracking follow-ups, working with calendars and documents, and asking the account owner for approval before consequential actions. This policy explains what information Gorlami accesses, how it is used, where it is stored, and the choices you have. Gorlami is operated by its developer (contact details at the end).

1. Information Gorlami accesses

Gorlami only accesses data from accounts that you connect yourself, through each provider's authorization screen (for Google accounts, Google OAuth). Depending on what you connect and the permissions you grant, this may include:

  • Email (Gmail): message headers, bodies, attachments, labels, and drafts in the connected mailbox, and the ability to create drafts, apply or remove labels, archive, and — only when you approve a specific message — send.
  • Calendar (Google Calendar): events and availability in the connected calendars, read-only unless you later grant write access.
  • Documents (Google Drive), when connected in a later version: file names, contents and metadata of files Gorlami is asked to find or read.
  • Account basics: the email address and display name of the connected account, used to label the connection in the app.
  • What you tell Gorlami: your messages in the app, rules and procedures you teach it, and your approvals and declines.

2. How the information is used

Gorlami uses this information for one purpose: to provide the service to the company that connected the account. Concretely, that means organizing and labeling mail according to your rules, drafting replies for your review, tracking commitments, proposing calendar options, finding documents you ask for, and showing you what was done and what needs your decision.

Gorlami does not sell your data, does not use it for advertising, does not build advertising profiles, and does not use your data to train general-purpose artificial-intelligence models.

3. Artificial-intelligence processing

To understand and act on your mail and instructions, Gorlami sends relevant content (for example, the text of an email thread and your rules) to a third-party AI model provider for processing. As of this policy's effective date that provider is Anthropic (Claude models), accessed through its commercial API, whose terms do not permit use of API inputs or outputs to train its models. Only the content needed for the specific task is sent, and credentials or authorization tokens are never included.

4. Where information is stored

In the current desktop version, Gorlami's working data — conversation history, tasks, procedures, approval records and cached summaries — is stored on your own computer, and authorization tokens are stored in the operating system's secure keychain. If and when a hosted version is offered, each company's data will be stored in an isolated environment for that company alone, and this policy will be updated to describe it before that version is used.

Gorlami does not keep a separate copy of your mailbox. It reads from the connected account when it needs to, and stores only the working records described above.

5. Sharing

Gorlami shares information only with: the AI model provider described in section 3, for processing; the service providers you connected (Google), to carry out the actions you have authorized; and, if legally required, authorities under a valid legal process. Gorlami does not share your data with any other third party.

6. Google user data and Limited Use

Gorlami's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular, Google user data is used only to provide or improve user-facing features of Gorlami that are visible to you; it is not transferred to others except as necessary to provide those features, for security, or to comply with law; it is not used for advertising; and humans do not read it except with your explicit permission, for security purposes, to comply with law, or when it has been aggregated and anonymized.

7. Your choices and control

  • You can disconnect any account at any time from Gorlami's Connectors screen, which deletes the stored authorization token.
  • You can also revoke Gorlami's access from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • You can export or delete Gorlami's local working data from its Settings screen.
  • You can view and change every rule Gorlami follows, and every action it takes is recorded and visible to you.

8. Retention

Working records are kept for as long as you use Gorlami with the connected account, or until you delete them. Disconnecting an account removes its token immediately; you may delete related working records at the same time.

9. Security

Authorization tokens are stored in the operating system keychain, never in plain files or in the content sent to the AI provider. Actions that leave the company — sending, deleting, spending, granting access — require your explicit approval, which shows exactly what will happen before you confirm. All external actions are logged.

10. Children

Gorlami is a business tool and is not directed at children under 18.

11. Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the effective date above will change and the current version will always be available at this address.
